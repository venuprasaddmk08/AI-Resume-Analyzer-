Backend Developer

About the role
We are looking for a Backend Developer to join our engineering team and help
build and scale our core REST API services.

Responsibilities
Design, build, and maintain REST APIs used by our web and mobile clients.
Write efficient SQL queries and maintain database schemas.
Collaborate with the DevOps team on deployment and infrastructure.

Requirements
2+ years of professional experience in backend development.
Strong proficiency in Python and experience with FastAPI or a similar framework.
Solid understanding of SQL and relational databases.
Experience building and consuming REST APIs.

Preferred Qualifications
Experience with Docker for containerizing services.
Experience with AWS cloud infrastructure.
Familiarity with Kubernetes for container orchestration.

Benefits
Remote-friendly work, health insurance, and a learning stipend.
